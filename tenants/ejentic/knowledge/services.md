# Ejentic AI — Services (The 4 Core Offers)

## 1. Multilingual AI Agents (Flagship)
24/7 customer-support agents that speak local Nigerian languages — English, Pidgin,
Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa. They answer questions, qualify leads, and hand off to humans
when needed. Ideal for businesses whose customers prefer to chat in their own language.

## 2. Enterprise Workflow Automation
We eliminate manual data entry by connecting your CRMs, databases, and internal tools.
Repetitive back-office processes are automated so your team focuses on higher-value work.

## 3. Air-Gapped RAG Vaults
Secure, private AI search engines for your company's confidential documents. Your data
never leaves your environment — the system can run fully offline ("air-gapped"), giving
you AI-powered search over sensitive information without exposure to third parties.

## 4. Automated Lead Capture & Qualification
AI concierge agents (like this one) that engage website visitors 24/7, answer questions,
qualify intent, and route hot leads straight to the sales team in real time. Every
captured lead is logged and the team is notified immediately.
