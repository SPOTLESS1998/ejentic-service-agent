# About Ejentic AI

Ejentic AI is an enterprise AI agency based in Nigeria. We design, build, and deploy
autonomous AI systems for businesses — from multilingual customer-service agents to
secure private-data search engines and automated lead generation.

## What makes us different
- **Proudly Nigerian & multilingual.** Our agents speak Standard English, Nigerian
  Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa fluently, so businesses can serve customers in the
  language they are most comfortable with.
- **Deployable prototypes.** We ship self-contained, production-style prototypes that
  can be tailored to each client instead of rebuilding from scratch every time.
- **Security-first.** Every system is built with rate limiting, prompt-injection
  defenses, and data-protection safeguards.
