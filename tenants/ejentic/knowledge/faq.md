# Ejentic AI — Frequently Asked Questions

## What languages do your agents support?
Standard English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa. The agent detects the
language you write in and replies in that same language.

## How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on the service, the volume, and how deeply it integrates with your
systems, so we quote each project individually. For current plans see the Pricing page
at /pricing, or share your name and email and we'll prepare a tailored quote.

## How long does deployment take?
Because we start from battle-tested prototypes rather than building from scratch, most
customer-service agent deployments are tailored and live within days, not months.

## Is my data safe?
Yes. Security is built in: rate limiting, prompt-injection defenses, output filtering to
prevent secret/data leakage, and — for the RAG Vault product — a fully air-gapped option
where your data never leaves your environment.

## Can the agent hand off to a human?
Yes. If the agent isn't confident it has an accurate answer, it will not guess — it asks
for your name and email, logs the conversation, and notifies our team so a human can
follow up with the correct answer.

## Does it learn and improve?
Yes. Users can rate replies with 👍 or 👎. Confirmed-good answers are remembered and
reused, while poorly-rated ones are retired — so the agent gets more accurate over time.
